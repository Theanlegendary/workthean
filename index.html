Programme Overview

The FIATA Higher International Diploma is designed for practitioners aiming to advance from operational logistics tasks to strategic supply chain decision-making. The curriculum addresses contemporary challenges in multimodal transportation, contract administration, customs clearance procedures, and supply chain financial control across Southeast Asian and global freight networks.

Participants develop practical case analysis skills to optimize end-to-end cargo movements, mitigate carrier liabilities, and design resilient supply chains aligned with international maritime and air conventions.

Curriculum (8 Core Modules • 160 Hours)

FIATA Minimum Standard Syllabus
Module 01 20 Guided Hours

Global Purchasing & International Procurement

Strategic supplier selection, purchasing contracts, vendor evaluation frameworks, and international procurement negotiation strategies.

Module 02 20 Guided Hours

Contract Management & Maritime Law

ICC Incoterms 2020 rules, passing of risk, letters of credit (UCP 600), international sales conventions (CISG), and standard logistics contract clauses.

Module 03 18 Guided Hours

Production & Operations Management

Lean logistics operations, material requirements planning (MRP), demand planning, and production scheduling coordination with cargo dispatch hubs.

Module 04 24 Guided Hours

Multimodal Transport & Freight Forwarding Operations

Issuance and liability under the negotiable FIATA Multimodal Bill of Lading (FBL), ocean container line bookings, air cargo airway bills, and sea-rail intermodal routing.

Module 05 16 Guided Hours

International Financial Management in Logistics

Cost accounting in transport operations, working capital management, currency exchange risk mitigation, and freight payment reconciliation.

Module 06 18 Guided Hours

Warehouse Operations & Inventory Optimization

Facility layout design, safety stock calculations, automated storage and retrieval systems (AS/RS), WMS technology, and cold-chain compliance.

Module 07 18 Guided Hours

Supply Chain Risk Management & Customs Compliance

WCO SAFE Framework, Authorized Economic Operator (AEO) requirements, customs audit protocols under regional commerce agreements, and cargo insurance subrogation.

Module 08 26 Guided Hours

Strategic Supply Chain Management & Capstone Project

Applied supply chain simulation addressing port delays, carrier disruptions, and logistics network re-engineering. Evaluated by an academic and industry panel.

Admission Requirements & Assessment

Prerequisites

  • Recognized Bachelor's degree, OR
  • Minimum 2 years of professional experience in freight forwarding, shipping, or logistics operations
  • Working proficiency in English for reading course modules and international case studies

Assessment Criteria

  • Continuous Module Assignments: 40%
  • Final FIATA Written Examination: 30%
  • Capstone Strategy Project & Defense: 30%
  • Minimum 65% aggregate score required for diploma issuance

Certification & Institutional Validation

Successful candidates are awarded the FIATA Higher International Diploma in Supply Chain Management. The certificate is issued under the authority of the International Federation of Freight Forwarders Associations (FIATA, Geneva) through its validated national association member, the Vietnam Logistics Research and Development Institute (VLI).

Graduates receive an individual verification registry number registered with FIATA's headquarters and are recognized across international freight associations worldwide.
